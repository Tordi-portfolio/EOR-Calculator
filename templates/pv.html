Pore Volume (PV)

Total void space within a reservoir rock available to store fluids, expressed in reservoir barrel units.

PV = (φ × A × L) / 5.615
φ Porosity (fraction)
A Cross-sectional area (ft²)
L Reservoir length / thickness (ft)
5.615 Conversion factor (ft³ → bbl)
Conversion 5.615 ft³/bbl
Bulk volume A × L
Output RB
